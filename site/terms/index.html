7. AI-Based Resume Parsing and Gemini Processing

GoCareer uses artificial intelligence technology to assist with automated resume parsing and Candidate profile creation. When a Candidate uploads a resume, the resume file and/or text extracted from the resume may be securely transmitted to Google Gemini or another authorized artificial intelligence service provider for processing.

Information sent for resume parsing may include

  • Candidate name, email address, phone number, and location information included in the resume
  • Professional summary, employment history, previous employers, job titles, and employment dates
  • Educational qualifications, institutions attended, skills, and competencies
  • Certifications, professional licenses, projects, achievements, and awards
  • Languages, portfolio information, professional profile links, and other information voluntarily included in the uploaded resume

The information is processed for purposes including

  • Extracting information from the uploaded resume
  • Converting unstructured resume information into structured data
  • Creating or updating the Candidate profile
  • Identifying Candidate skills and professional experience
  • Supporting job matching functionality and generating job recommendations
  • Calculating or supporting Candidate-job match scores
  • Improving search and discovery functionality
  • Providing other AI-assisted recruitment features available through GoCareer

GoCareer only sends information necessary to provide the relevant AI-powered resume parsing and processing functionality. Account passwords, OTPs, authentication credentials, and other unrelated Platform information are not sent to the AI service for resume parsing. AI-generated or automatically extracted information may not always be complete, accurate, or error-free. Candidates are responsible for reviewing their parsed information and making corrections where necessary. The processing of information by Google Gemini or other third-party AI service providers may also be subject to the applicable provider’s terms, privacy policies, data processing terms, and security practices. By uploading a resume and using the AI-based resume processing functionality, the Candidate acknowledges and agrees that information contained in the uploaded resume may be processed by authorized third-party AI service providers for the purposes described above.
